## 10. Important interpretation notes

### Historical vs forecast

Historical values are sourced from company disclosures. Forecast values
are model assumptions or formulas based on those assumptions.

### Management guidance vs analyst assumptions

The model deliberately distinguishes company-reported information from
analyst assumptions. Forecast capacity, utilisation, realisation growth,
EBITDA/tonne growth, capex, working capital, debt issuance and dividend
payout should not be described as UltraTech guidance.

### Reporting-definition consistency

The model uses statutory Revenue from Operations consistently for the
historical series. UltraTech's management presentation of Net Sales can
differ from statutory Revenue from Operations; those definitions should
not be mixed without explanation.

### FY24 restatement

UltraTech restated its financial results following the Kesoram
transaction, with the appointed date of 1 April 2024. The model uses the
appropriate restated comparative figures.

### Model purpose

This is a learning and portfolio model intended to demonstrate financial
modelling methodology, statement integration and valuation mechanics. It
is not intended to reproduce UltraTech's internal planning model or
professional sell-side research model.
