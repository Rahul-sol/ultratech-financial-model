## Valuation inputs

Risk-free rate, equity risk premium, market capitalisation and beta sources are recorded in the workbook's `11_Sources` sheet, together with the relevant source references and model-date assumptions.

## Data treatment notes

- Historical statements use consolidated statutory Revenue from Operations consistently.
- FY24 comparatives reflect the relevant restatement following the Kesoram transaction.
- Management Net Sales and statutory Revenue from Operations are not mixed without explanation.
- Forecast assumptions are independent analyst/model assumptions and are not presented as company guidance.
